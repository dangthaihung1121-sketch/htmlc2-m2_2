Messi vs Ronaldo: 2003 to 2020

Meet the Players 2003-2020 2021-2026

Early Careers (2003-2009)

Ronaldo joined Manchester United in 2003 and became a global star there. He won the Champions League and his first Ballon d'Or in 2008. Messi made his Barcelona debut in 2004 at age 17 and won his first Ballon d'Or in 2009. He also won Olympic gold with Argentina in 2008.

The Great Rivalry (2009-2018)

In 2009 Ronaldo moved to Real Madrid, and the two met in El Clasico about 35 times. Messi won four Ballon d'Or awards in a row (2009-2012) and scored 73 goals in the 2011-12 season. Ronaldo answered with 450 goals in 438 games for Real Madrid and four Champions League titles with the club (2014, 2016, 2017, 2018).

  • Ronaldo won Euro 2016 and the 2019 Nations League with Portugal
  • Messi lost the 2014 World Cup final and had no senior international title yet
  • Ronaldo moved to Juventus in 2018; Messi won his sixth Ballon d'Or in 2019
